Was it the price, or the person?

Every quote is compared with its customer's own rate card: base + (miles − included) × rate per mile. That splits quotes into ones where somebody made a decision and ones where they didn't.

illustrative The split is the design; the percentages need the full pricing run. What is already measured: 34% of quotes reproduce the tariff price to the penny, and the median quote sits +1.7% from tariff — so the model is unbiased and the spread is real pricing behaviour, not noise.

How this fits the main dashboard

Three levels, so the front page stays readable and the depth is one click away.

1 · Front page

One line in the weekly verdict: “Conversion 36.9%, down 2 pts. A third of quotes went out at tariff — 7.5T and Small Van Regional are losing at the rate card.” Plus the £500+ band as a single tile.

2 · Quotes tab

Everything on this page: the funnel, the price-or-person split, rate-card health, the desk table, value bands and cancellation reasons.

3 · Drill-down

Click a person or a tariff for the quote-level list — customer, distance, tariff price, quoted price, outcome — so a 1:1 has the actual examples in front of it.
